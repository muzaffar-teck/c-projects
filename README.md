# 🏦 Banking System in C

A **console-based banking system** project developed in C.  
🏆 Awarded **1st prize at the department level** for its functionality and design.

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ **Create & manage bank accounts**  
- ✅ **Deposit & Withdraw money** with preset and custom amounts  
- ✅ **Transfer money** between accounts  
- ✅ **Apply and repay loans** (partial or full)  
- ✅ **Transaction history** for users  
- ✅ **Admin panel** to:  
  - View all accounts  
  - Block or unblock accounts  
  - Approve loan requests  
  - View transactions  
- ✅ **Notifications** for loan approval and repayments
